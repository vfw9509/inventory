Inventory count

Walk each storage spot and scan every bottle, can or case (each scan adds one), or type the number into the checklist. Partial bottles: 0.5. Kegs and syrup boxes: count only the full ones; the open one is tracked by sales. When you finish, the counts replace what's on the sheet. Items you skip stay the same.

Count while the bar is closed: before it opens (on event days, before the event starts) or after close. Drinks poured during a count make it look like bottles are missing.

Drink recipes

For pitchers, mixed drinks and plates that use more than one item, or more than one pour: a pitcher is 4 pints of its keg, a rum and Coke is a shot of rum plus a drink from the Coke box.

Take sales off inventory by hand

Only for days the daily email didn't cover. In Lighthouse, run Sales Summary by Item for one day and export it. Then load the file here and check the day. For trends, you can pick many old one-day reports at once: they go to the sales history only, with the day read from each file name.

Send barcodes to SkyTab

Downloads a CSV of SkyTab item names with their UPCs and costs, to update menu items in SkyTab.

Items

Add items from a spreadsheet

Works with the starter list, SkyTab's Menu Item Detail export, or any spreadsheet with a Name column.

Categories

Add, rename, reorder or remove categories. Renaming one changes it on every item.

Storage spots

Where things are kept, in the order you walk them, with a photo of each. The count checklist groups items by spot, and a count can cover one spot.

Vendors

Who you buy from: contact, phone, website, account number. Put each item's price at each vendor on the item; the order list puts it under the cheapest.

Shelf labels & door sheets

Shelf labels: each item's name, barcode and par on Avery 18160 (or 5160) address labels, 30 to a sheet. Door sheets: a full page for the front of a cooler or freezer, listing what's kept inside.

Bulk edit in Excel

Download every item to a spreadsheet, change storage spots, pars, vendor prices, on-hand counts and more in Excel, then upload it. You see every change before anything is saved.

Copy from demo mode

Storage spots, categories or vendors you set up in demo mode on this device can be copied into the Google Sheet.

Demo mode

For trying the app with made-up items. Nothing done in demo mode reaches the Google Sheet, and this device stops using the sheet until you leave demo mode.
