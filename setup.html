Bar Inventory Scanner: Setup

Free for any VFW Post. Scan bottles with a phone. Counts go into a Google Sheet you own.

0 of 11 steps done

Before you start

  • Time: about 45 minutes. You can stop and come back. This page remembers where you were.
  • Cost: free. No credit card.
  • You need: a computer (much easier than a phone for setup), a Google account, and your phone for the last steps.
  • Tip: make a Gmail just for your Post (like vfwpost1234@gmail.com). Then the inventory doesn't disappear when one person leaves.
  • How it works: your Post's data lives in your Google Sheet. Your app lives on your free GitHub page. Nobody else can see or change your numbers.

3

Add the inventory code to your sheet

5 min
  1. In your Google Sheet, click the menu Extensions → Apps Script. A code editor opens in a new tab.
  1. Click inside the code box. Press Ctrl+A (Mac: ⌘+A) to select everything, then press Delete. The box is now empty.
  1. Come back to this page and click this button:
  1. Go back to the code editor tab. Click in the empty box and press Ctrl+V (Mac: ⌘+V) to paste.
  2. Click the 💾 Save icon above the code.
  3. Next to the ▷ Run button, there is a dropdown menu. Pick setup from it.
  4. Click ▷ Run.

Google will now ask for permission. This is normal. Google shows this warning for any code it didn't write itself:

  1. Click Review permissions, then click your Google account.
  2. You'll see "Google hasn't verified this app." Click Advanced (small link at the bottom left).
  3. Click Go to Untitled project (unsafe). It says "unsafe" only because you added the code yourself.
  4. Click Allow.
The code only works on this one spreadsheet. It also looks up product names online when you scan a new bottle.

At the bottom of the code editor: Execution completed. Back in your spreadsheet, two new tabs at the bottom: Items and Log.

Stuck? Common problems
  • "Syntax error": part of the code is missing or there's extra code. Go back to step 2 of this section, delete everything, and paste again.
  • I don't see "setup" in the dropdown: click 💾 Save first, then look again.
  • No Items/Log tabs: refresh the spreadsheet tab (press F5).
4

Turn your sheet into a web app

3 min

This gives your sheet a web address that the phones can talk to.

  1. In the code editor tab, click the blue Deploy button (top right) → New deployment.
  1. Next to Select type, click the ⚙️ gear → Web app.
  1. Fill in:
    • Description: inventory
    • Execute as: Me
    • Who has access: Anyone. Not "Anyone with Google account".
  2. Click Deploy. If Google asks for permission again, do the same Advanced → Go to … → Allow as before.
  3. Under Web app URL, click Copy.
Is "Anyone" safe? Yes. It only means people don't need a Google login to use the app. Nobody can see or change anything without your PIN, and your spreadsheet stays private.

Turn on your app's web page

3 min + 2 min wait
  1. In your inventory repository, click ⚙️ Settings (in the row of tabs near the top).
  2. In the list on the left, click Pages.
  3. Under Build and deployment, make sure Source says Deploy from a branch.
  4. Under Branch, change None to main. Leave / (root). Click Save.
  5. Wait 2 minutes, then refresh the page (F5).

A box at the top that says "Your site is live at …"

Stuck? Common problems
  • No "Pages" in the list: you're in your account settings. Go back to the inventory repository and use its Settings tab.
  • Branch menu has no "main", or it says you need to upgrade: the repository is Private. In Settings → General, scroll to the bottom (Danger Zone) → Change visibility → Public.
  • "404 There isn't a GitHub Pages site here": wait a few more minutes. Then check that index.html is in the main list of files, not inside a folder.

10

Add your bottles and do your first count

30–60 min at the bar

First, get your list of items into the app. Pick one:

  • Easiest: our starter list. It has about 75 common liquors, beers, wines and sodas. Download the starter list. Then, on a computer, open your app → More → Import items → choose the file → untick anything you don't carry → Add items.
  • From SkyTab: in SkyTab, export the Menu Item Detail report to Excel. Then open your app → More → Import items → choose that file. Leave food unticked.
  • Start from nothing: skip this. Scan each bottle and the app asks you to name it.

Then do your first full count (this sets the real numbers):

  1. Open the app → Count tab → Start a count.
  2. Scan every bottle, can and case. Each scan adds 1. A case barcode adds the whole case.
  3. The first time you scan a product, the app asks about the barcode. Tap Add to existing item, find it in the list, and say if it's a single or a case. After that, the barcode is known forever.
  4. For part-full bottles, type a number like 0.5.
  5. When you're done, tap Save count.
  6. Set a Par (how many to keep on hand) on each item: Items tab → tap an item → Edit item. You can also type pars in the Google Sheet's Par column.
11

Give it to your bartenders

5 min

Print a card to keep behind the bar. Bartenders scan the QR code with their phone camera, type their name and the staff PIN, and they're set.

Leave the PIN off if the card will be where members can see it. Tell bartenders the PIN in person instead.

🎉 Your weekly routine

  • Delivery comes in: Scan → + Received. Scan a case barcode to add the whole case.
  • Broken, comped, or moved to the canteen: Scan → − Used / removed, and pick a reason.
  • Once or twice a week: Count tab → scan everything → Save count.
  • Before ordering: the Order tab shows everything below par. Tap Text / copy list to send it to your supplier.
  • Once a week (SkyTab): in SkyTab, run Reports → Sales Summary by Item for the days since your last import, and export it to Excel. In the app, go to More → Import a SkyTab sales report. Always count first, then import only the sales from after that count.
  • Mistake? Every change has an Undo button on the Scan tab, and the Google Sheet's Log tab shows who did what.

Questions

How do I change a PIN later?
  1. Open your Google Sheet → Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Near the top, change the numbers inside the quotes on the STAFF_PIN or MANAGER_PIN line. Click 💾 Save.
  3. Click Deploy → Manage deployments → ✏️ pencil → under Version pick New version → Deploy. Your web address stays the same.
  4. Everyone types the new PIN in the app (More tab).
We got SkyTab later (or stopped using it). How do I turn that on or off?

Come back to this page. In step 1, change your answer and click Save. In step 6, download your files again. Then in GitHub, use Add file → Upload files to upload just the new index.html and click Commit. Phones get the change the next time they open the app.

Can I get my inventory into Excel?

Yes. In the app: More → Download inventory (Excel). Or, in the Google Sheet: File → Download → Microsoft Excel.

What if there's no cell signal in the cooler?

Keep scanning. The app saves changes on the phone and sends them when you're back in signal. The label at the top shows how many are waiting.

Who can see our numbers?

Only people with access to your Google Sheet, and people who have your PINs (through the app). The GitHub page only holds the app itself, not your data.
